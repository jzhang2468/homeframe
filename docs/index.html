    EXPERIMENT PROTOCOL

    Learning a home.

    At spawn, robots know their role and starting dock. The apartment layout, chores and other robots are discovered through local sensing.

    01 / Perceive
    A robot sees a finite cone. Walls and selected furniture occlude its sensors. It labels free space, obstacles, chores and other robots on its own map.
    02 / Decide
    TIDY prefers efficient collection and delivery. CARE prioritizes urgent cleaning and returns to charge between jobs. Both crews can interrupt a search when battery is low. Nearby robots yield or detour at conflicts.
    03 / Remember
    Doorways, docks and chore locations can become landmarks. With retention enabled, a robot can retrieve them to plan a later trip.
    04 / Compare
    Inspect one robot’s knowledge, or use Trails and Traffic to see the combined paths and passage use. Save setups A and B to compare different chore placements and robot parameters. You can also test memory on and off for the active setup.

    This is a behavior model in a designed floor plan. Sensor ranges, service times and battery use are simulation parameters.
